PawGuard: Privacy and Refund Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Privacy

PawGuard is a web app that gives general guidance on whether the weather is suitable for walking your dog. It has no accounts and no server that stores your personal data.

  • Your dog's profile. Breed, age group and city are saved only on your own device, in your browser. We never receive them.
  • Location. If you tap the pin, your phone asks for permission. Your coordinates (or the city you type) are sent to Open-Meteo to get the weather forecast. We do not store them.
  • Analytics. We use Umami to count visits and actions, such as "checked a dog" or "switched language", along with approximate country, device type and browser. It is designed to work without cookies, and we do not use it to identify you.
  • Payments. Founding Member payments are handled by Stripe. We never see your card number. Stripe shares purchase details such as your name and email with us so we can serve you.
  • Alerts. If you turn on notifications, they are created on your device.
  • Affiliate links. Some product links may earn us a commission at no extra cost to you.
  • Children. PawGuard is not directed to children under 13.

To erase what is stored on your device, clear your browser data for this site or remove the app from your Home Screen. For any privacy question, write to us at the email below.

Refund policy

Founding Member is an early-access purchase. Pro features are still being built, and members get access when they launch.

  • You can ask for a full refund within 30 days of your purchase. No reason needed.
  • Email us from the address you used to pay, and we will refund the original payment method. Your bank may take several days to show it.

Not veterinary advice

PawGuard gives general guidance only. If your dog shows heavy panting, drooling, weakness or collapse, call a veterinarian right away.
